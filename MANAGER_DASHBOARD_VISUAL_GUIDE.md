## 🎨 COLOR SCHEME

- **Gold (#D4AF37)**: Primary actions, highlights
- **Green (#10b981)**: Revenue, success, approvals
- **Blue (#3b82f6)**: Orders, information
- **Purple (#8b5cf6)**: Tables, premium features
- **Orange (#f59e0b)**: Pending items, warnings
- **Red (#ef4444)**: Alerts, cancellations, critical

---

## 🔔 NOTIFICATION SYSTEM

### Alert Types
1. **🔴 Warning (Red)**
   - Delayed orders (30+ minutes)
   - Payment issues
   - Critical staff alerts

2. **🔵 Info (Blue)**
   - Pending feedback responses
   - Shift change requests
   - General notifications

### Notification Badge
- Shows count on Bell icon
- Auto-updates every 30 seconds
- Click to view details
